Daily Affirmations & Gratitude

A simple daily habit app for supportive affirmations, gratitude prompts, journaling, quiet reflection, and mental resilience.

Learn about the app

A Quiet Reflection Practice

Choose from built-in and public affirmation sets, create local sets on your Android device, or sign in with a verified email address to back up private sets. You can also choose from different gratitude prompts and write journal entries in the app, keeping affirmation practice and gratitude reflection together in one calm daily routine.

App Screenshots

Daily Affirmations & Gratitude keeps the core flows simple: start a session, choose the reflection you need, write what you are grateful for, and follow your progress over time.

Features

  • Built-in affirmation content.
  • Public affirmation sets available to everyone.
  • Local custom sets saved on your device.
  • Verified-account private sets backed up with Firebase.
  • Optional publishing of user-created affirmation sets.
  • Choice of gratitude prompts for guided reflection.
  • Gratitude journal writing inside the app.
  • Offline-friendly behavior through bundled content and local caches.
  • Curated visual themes, reduced motion, and high-contrast readability options.
